About Catchy Decors — Karur's Home Finishing Studio

20+ years of experience, 1000+ homes styled, and a team that handles every step from measurement to installation.

Catchy Decors was built on a simple idea: every home deserves finishing touches that feel personal. For more than 20 years, we've worked with families across Karur to bring curtains, blinds, wallpapers, headboards and flooring together into interiors that truly feel like home. As a full-service interior decoration and home interior products studio, we also work with apartment owners, builders, offices, shops and hotels across Karur.

Led by R. Babu, our team has completed over 1000 projects — from single-room curtain installations to full home interior makeovers. We work directly with trusted brands including D'Decor, GM Enterprises, Asian Paints, AD Blinds, Livin Blinds, Asian Blinds, Johnson Blinds and Excel Wallpaper, so every material we recommend is one we genuinely stand behind.

What sets Catchy Decors apart is how we work: we visit your home, understand your space, your light, and your everyday life, and guide you toward choices that are both beautiful and practical. From the first measurement to the final fitting, our own team handles installation — so what you see in the showroom is exactly what ends up on your walls and windows.
